Short-form vertical videos on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts give you only a brief window to communicate clearly. When editing promotional or informational clips, structure matters just as much as transitions.

## 1. Open With Context Early

Avoid long intro cards or slow fades. Opening directly with the main question, key visual, or spoken point helps viewers understand what the clip is about right away.

## 2. Respect Platform Safe Zones

On 9:16 screens, the bottom edge and right-hand column are often covered by captions, profile handles, and platform buttons. Keeping titles and subtitles centered within the safe frame keeps text readable.

## 3. Edit for Sound-Off and Sound-On Viewing

Many users scroll with audio muted at first. Pairing clean voice audio cleanup with concise on-screen text ensures the core message still lands either way.
