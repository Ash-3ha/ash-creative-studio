Managing website content and landing pages is not just about publishing text—it is about making pages easy for visitors to read and straightforward for search engines to crawl.

## 1. Clear Heading Structure

Every landing page should have:

- One clear `<h1>` that matches the page's core topic or campaign intent.
- Logical `<h2>` and `<h3>` sections so visitors scanning on mobile can grasp the page quickly.

## 2. Unique Metadata & Canonical Tags

Whenever I add or update a page through the CMS, I check:

- **Title tag:** Specific to the page, clear, and without generic boilerplate.
- **Meta description:** Honest summary aligned with what is actually on the page.
- **Canonical URL:** Points cleanly to the live trailing-slash URL.
- **Image alt text:** Descriptive text for every meaningful visual.

## 3. Mobile Layout & Form Usability

Most paid social and local search traffic arrives on mobile screens. Before wrapping up any page update, I test button spacing, sticky elements, form validation messages, and load speed on smaller viewports.
