A name is the shortest possible brand story. For creative studios, finding a moniker that achieves domain availability, international memorability, and category longevity is one of the hardest strategic exercises.

## The Pitfalls of Modern Agency Naming

1. **The Keyword Mashup:** Names like "DigitalGrowthMedia" date rapidly and pigeonhole capabilities.
2. **The Latin Word Game:** Selecting abstract Latin roots often produces sterile corporate identities lacking warmth.
3. **The Two-Noun Template:** "Fox & Arrow", "Wolf & Anchor" had their moment in 2014, but are now indistinguishable tropes.

## Why Brevity Wins

Short, punchy monikers like **ASH** provide an anchor: they act as a sharp visual stamp, allow prominent monograms, and leave ample space for the actual work to define the emotional tone of the studio.
