## [Sample Case Study Overview]

> *Note: This is a sample portfolio case study illustrating the editorial case study layout for ASH Creative Studio.*

Vantage Capital manages mid-market private equity investments. Their digital presence needed to reflect both financial gravitas and modern tech agility.

### Architecture & Design

We developed a restrained, editorial design framework rooted in dark charcoal palettes and crisp typography. The platform was built with static-site efficiency to guarantee maximum uptime, security, and instantaneous load times for institutional investors worldwide.
