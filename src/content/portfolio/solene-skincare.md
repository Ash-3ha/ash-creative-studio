## [Sample Case Study Overview]

> *Note: This is a sample portfolio case study illustrating the editorial case study layout for ASH Creative Studio.*

Solene Skincare required a comprehensive identity transformation to match their premium organic formulation standards. Our studio provided full-cycle creative direction from initial brand strategy through to physical packaging mockups and digital guidelines.

### The Challenge

In an oversaturated beauty marketplace, standing out without relying on generic greenwashing cues required a sophisticated design strategy. The brand needed to appeal to high-net-worth consumers seeking scientific efficacy packaged in quiet luxury.

### Our Approach

1. **Brand Discovery & Positioning:** Establishing tone of voice, visual metaphors, and competitive differentiation.
2. **Identity Architecture:** Developing an iconic monogram supported by custom editorial serifs.
3. **Packaging Engineering:** Working within sustainable material constraints to achieve tactile luxury.
4. **Digital Translation:** Ensuring seamless cross-platform consistency for web and social channels.

### The Deliverables

The engagement yielded a production-ready brand manual including packaging dielines, photography art direction, and digital asset toolkits.
