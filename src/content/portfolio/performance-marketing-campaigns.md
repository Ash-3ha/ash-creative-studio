**The starting point.** People comparing hospitals visit a website with one question: can I reach someone? The useful visits end in a phone call, a WhatsApp message or a form. The aim was to bring more of those visitors in through paid search and social, and to see which ads and keywords delivered them.

**How the campaigns work.** Google Ads search campaigns meet people at the moment they look for care in Coimbatore. Meta Ads campaigns reach defined local audiences with image and video creatives. I set up the campaigns, choose the audiences, write the ad copy and watch the budgets. Because this is healthcare, the copy stays factual and promises no outcomes.

**Tracking every route in.** Google Tag Manager records form submissions, WhatsApp clicks, phone calls and thank-you page views. GA4 shows what visitors do after the click, and Search Console shows how the site appears in organic search. I also make website updates through its CMS.

**Creative and optimisation.** I design the ad visuals and edit the video variations. When a campaign lags, I look for the gap in targeting, copy or creative and change it. I follow leads from enquiry toward conversion rather than stopping at the form fill.

**What this shows.** Performance marketing for a hospital is a measurement job as much as an advertising job. Reliable tracking comes first, then decisions made on enquiries.
