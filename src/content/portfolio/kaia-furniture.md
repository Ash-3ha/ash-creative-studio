## [Sample Case Study Overview]

> *Note: This is a sample portfolio case study illustrating the editorial case study layout for ASH Creative Studio.*

Kaia Furniture designs bespoke architectural seating and cabinetry. Launching a catalog across multiple finishes and fabrics traditionally required hundreds of thousands of dollars in studio photography.

### The Challenge

Creating digital assets indistinguishable from physical studio photography, capturing subtle textile fuzz, anisotropic reflections on brushed metals, and realistic fabric folds.

### Execution & Craft

Every model was constructed using precision CAD references, followed by custom sculpting for organic cushion wrinkles and seam imperfections. Rendering under multi-bounce global illumination delivered the depth required for luxury print and web applications.
