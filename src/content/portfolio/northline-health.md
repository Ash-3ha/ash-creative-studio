## [Sample Case Study Overview]

> *Note: This is a sample portfolio case study illustrating the editorial case study layout for ASH Creative Studio.*

Northline Health needed a performance marketing framework that strictly adhered to medical advertising guidelines while maintaining high conversion velocity.

### Strategy & Implementation

We rebuilt their acquisition architecture from the ground up: high-intent search queries were isolated into exact and phrase match structures, while remarketing was configured safely through first-party data capture. Every conversion touchpoint was routed through Google Tag Manager with server-side validation.
