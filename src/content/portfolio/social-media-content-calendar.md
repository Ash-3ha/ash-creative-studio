**The starting point.** People rarely act on a hospital's social post until they trust the hospital. A steady feed needs a plan that earns that trust first, and asks for action later.

**How the calendar is built.** The 90 days run in three monthly phases. Trust & Awareness comes first, then Clinical Authority, then Patient Acquisition. Thirty-six anchor pieces hold the plan together, and a bank of 110 ideas across six categories gives room to adapt as the weeks go on.

**What goes into it.** Informational posts and carousels that explain one topic at a time, awareness campaign creatives, and short videos. I design the visuals, edit the videos, write the captions and format the hashtags, so every piece looks and reads like it belongs to the same feed. Everything is planned within the NMC advertising guidelines.

**What this shows.** Social media for a hospital works best planned like a campaign: trust first, then expertise, then enquiries.
